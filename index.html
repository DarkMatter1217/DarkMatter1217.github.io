Research

Making RAG answers checkable, one claim at a time

RAG systems usually trust whatever they retrieve. My system, ARA, splits an answer into separate claims and checks each one against independent evidence from the web. If it can't find support, it says so instead of guessing.

A Claim-Centric Multi-Source Verification Architecture for Reducing Hallucinations in Retrieval-Augmented Generation
